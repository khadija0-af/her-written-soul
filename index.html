The Soul's Horizon

The sun descends in silent prayer,
A golden weight upon the air.
The clouds are etched in lines of fire,
A burning ghost of deep desire.

In every shade of rose and ink,
Upon this wide and hollow brink,
The world exhales and finds its rest,
Within the light of the crimson west.

For here, where day and night collide,
The secrets of the soul reside.
In the quiet glow of the dying flame,
Whispering winds speak every name.

— A Soul Written In Ink
